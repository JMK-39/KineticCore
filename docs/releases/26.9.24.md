# KineticCore 26.9.24

## 简体中文

- 扩展 `ScrollableItemGrid` 的通用业务状态描边：新增 `ItemGridOutline.SUCCESS` 和 `ItemGridOutline.WARNING`。
- 物品网格的成功/警告状态统一使用主题描边，不再显示角标；以 `ItemGridOutline` 和单一轮廓字段替换旧角标状态 API。
- 状态优先级为错误红框、悬停蓝框、选中框、业务状态框、默认白框。描边颜色由 KineticCore 主题管理。
- 不包含任何附属模组专属数据或玩法逻辑。

## English

- Extended reusable `ScrollableItemGrid` business-state outlines with `ItemGridOutline.SUCCESS` and `ItemGridOutline.WARNING`.
- Success and warning states now use theme-owned slot outlines instead of corner badges. `ItemGridOutline` and a single outline field replace the former corner-marker state API.
- Outline priority is error (red), hover (blue), selection, business state, then the default white outline. KineticCore owns the outline colors through its theme.
- No addon-specific data or gameplay behavior is included.
